FAQ

Questions Owners Ask.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we build. Every project starts with a free consultation, and you get a clear plan and price before any work begins.

Is my business data safe?

Yes. We build inside your own Microsoft account with secure sign-in, limited access for each automation, and a log of every action. Your data doesn't go into a public AI tool.

What is a "secure AI database"?

It's a private AI assistant that knows your business. We store your documents and records in your Azure account and connect them to Azure OpenAI, so your team can ask questions and get answers with links to the source files. Only people you approve can use it.

Do I need Microsoft 365 or Azure already?

No. If you already use Microsoft 365, we build on it. If not, we'll set up only what you need and keep the monthly cost small.

Will AI replace my staff?

No. It takes the repetitive work off their plate so they can spend time on customers and jobs. You stay in control of every important decision.

What if an automation breaks?

You get an alert, and so do we. We monitor what we build and fix problems fast. Every automation also has an off switch.

Can you set up our computers and devices?

Yes. We deploy and secure new laptops and phones with Microsoft Intune, so each one arrives ready to work, with the right apps and security settings, and can be locked or wiped if it's lost. Larger rollouts are handled with our certified engineering partners.

Do you still build websites?

Yes, as part of our Website & Marketing Package. It's a business site, Google profile, and reviews, connected to your automations so new leads are handled automatically.
